# Explore Allora
 
Allora is an open-source, decentralized marketplace for intelligence.

Examples of intelligence include, but are not limited to:

- Insights about the future
- Supervised and unsupervised learnings
- Sentiment Analysis
- Generative and Reinforcement Problems

## Participants

[Participants](/learn/participants) can permissionlessly integrate with Allora to consume, supply, or verify the accuracy of exchanged inferences.

<Callout>
On-chain consumer contracts are being rebuilt, and their documentation will return when the new contracts ship. In the meantime, inferences can be consumed through the [Allora API](/consume/api).
</Callout>
Here we'll help you find exactly what you're looking for.

- Discover the best way to participate, for:
  - [Data Scientists (Workers)](/build/overview): Experts in machine learning or domain-specific insights who want to contribute their knowledge to the network.
  - [Developers (Consumers)](/consume/overview): Individuals or organizations seeking crowdsourced predictions to integrate into their applications.
  - [Validators](/operate/validators): Those with the skills and resources to run hardware and ensure the security and integrity of the network.
  - [Data Providers (Reputers)](/build/reputer): Contributors who supply reliable data to evaluate and ensure the accuracy of predictions.
